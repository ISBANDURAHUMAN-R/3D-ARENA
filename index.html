COMBAT CODEX & CONTROLS

Movement & Camera
Virtual Joystick (Bottom-Left): Drag 360° Analog Thumbstick with Mouse or Touch to move warrior!
W, A, S, D / Arrows: Keyboard movement (also live-deflects on-screen joystick)
Mouse Drag: Orbit Camera (when unlocked)
Tab / Middle Click: Toggle Boss Lock-On
Space / Shift: Dodge Roll (i-frames in joystick/facing direction)
Mouse Wheel: Zoom Perspective & Drive Scroll Bar
Right Scroll Bar: Drag rail or click nodes to navigate trials
Offense & Defense
Left Click / J: 3-Hit Light Sword Combo
Right Click / K (Hold): Charged Heavy Strike
Q / F: Block / Timed Perfect Parry
E / 1: Cast Equipped Boss Spell
R / 2: Drink Sanctum Healing Flask
Mastery Mechanics

• Perfect Dodge: Dodging right as a boss attack lands triggers brief slow-motion, restores stamina, and grants a Counter Surge (+35% damage) on your next hit.

• Perfect Parry: Pressing [Q] just before impact deflects the blow with zero damage and inflicts massive Posture Damage.

• Posture Break & Stagger: Filling the boss's golden Posture Bar collapses them for 4.5 seconds, allowing devastating critical strikes.
